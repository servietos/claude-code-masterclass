# Lessons

## Proposal 1: name the structure, promoted

Naming the three headings in the skill took the sections metric from 1 of 9 heading checks to
9 of 9. A format the skill only implies is a suggestion; a format it names is a constraint the
model follows on every run.

## Proposal 2: a ceiling means the next grader comes first, promoted

After v1 every grader sat at 3 of 3, yet every answer ended with a `---` rule and a paragraph
about the commits it left out. The defect was visible and unmeasured, so the cycle started by
writing `notes-only`, then re-measured the unchanged v1 skill as v1b before proposing anything.
That baseline (0 of 3) is what made the v2 result (3 of 3) attributable to the skill rather than
to the new grader.
