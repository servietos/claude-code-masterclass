# Process Notes: A Week at the Counter

Recorded by Mara Huber (Shop Owner) during one week of rentals, 5 to 9 October 2026.

| Day | Step | Tool | Minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | A customer rents a city bike for three days; I write the rental into the Rentals sheet and take a 50 euro cash deposit | Excel, cash box | 6 |
| Mon | The rental is also typed into the shop app so the bike shows as rented | Shop app | 4 |
| Tue | I call every customer whose bike is due tomorrow, from the Due column, to remind them | Phone, Excel | 25 |
| Wed | A bike comes back one day late; I read the Late Fee column in Excel and take the fee with the deposit refund | Excel, cash box | 8 |
| Wed | I mark the bike returned in the shop app | Shop app | 3 |
| Thu | I check the Service sheet for bikes whose next service date has passed and pull them off the floor by hand | Excel | 15 |
| Fri | I count the late fees of the week from the Rentals sheet for the owner report | Excel | 20 |

What costs the most: the reminder calls, because nothing tells me who is due tomorrow except filtering the sheet. Deposits are never written into the shop app, so the cash box and the Rentals sheet are the only record.
